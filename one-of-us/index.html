Welcome to Your Resource Hub

Being autistic in a neurotypical world comes with unique challenges. This space is designed to help you develop skills that support your wellbeing, advocate for your needs, and communicate in ways that feel authentic to you.

Remember: These are tools and suggestions, not rules. Take what resonates and leave what doesn't.

Additional Resources

Crisis Support:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988)
  • Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741)
  • Autistic Self Advocacy Network (ASAN) resources

Finding Community:

  • Local autism support groups (many are online now)
  • Autistic-led organizations and advocacy groups
  • Online communities on Reddit, Discord, and other platforms
  • Social media hashtags: #ActuallyAutistic, #AskingAutistics

Learning More:

  • Seek out autistic creators and authors (not just about autism)
  • Read experiences from diverse autistic adults
  • Follow autistic-led research and advocacy
  • Remember: autistic experiences are diverse - no single story represents everyone

You belong exactly as you are.

These resources are starting points, not rules. Your journey with autism is your own. Take what helps, adapt what needs adapting, and trust yourself to know what you need.

You are one of us, and we're glad you're here.
