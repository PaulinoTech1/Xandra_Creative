About Me

Xandra the Creative

I'm a video creator shaped by a love for expressing myself unfettered. I approach video making with a curiosity for where my mind can take me, and so I freestyle all of my videos.

What does that mean? It means that I don't conceptualize most of my ideas verbally before I create. I'll take an initial video for a hook and then go into CapCut to see where my creative process takes me: sans AI.

Although this mode of expression has fueled some speculation that my creations are AI, they are not. I'm autistic and what I refer to as my "hero skill" is expressing myself visually. And so visual communication is how I share the contents of my soul with the world and videos are my favorite way of doing that.

Autism, for me, is about finding depth beyond my limitations. How I commit fully to observing my inner self and fixate on honing the creative expression that helps me understand who I am.

Who am I beyond video making? Well, I'm a dog mom. I once wrote a poem about myself through my dog's eyes, which is probably the most honest biography I'll ever produce.

Beyond dog momming and making weird vids, I love to help others. I've coached for Special Olympics as well as volunteered my time visiting hospice patients. I like to know that I've made someone's day better in some way (special mention to people who say my content has helped or inspired them!)

And… I believe self-ownership is a form of self-advocacy, and that creativity doesn't need permission to exist. My work is for people who enjoy nuance, tenderness, and looking twice.
